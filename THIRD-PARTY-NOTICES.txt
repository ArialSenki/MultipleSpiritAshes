This file identifies third-party software dependencies and reproduces the license notices selected for this release. The package inventory is based on Cargo.lock and Cargo metadata for the x86_64-pc-windows-msvc build. It includes transitive and build-time dependencies; being listed does not mean a crate is linked into the final DLL.
